# Pricing & Care Structures — Pure Heart Home Health Care
> Serving Chicago, Rockford, and Northern Illinois Families with Transparent, Flexible In-Home Care.

## Operating Model Overview
Pure Heart Home Health Care operates as a licensed non-medical home care agency on a transparent private-pay and Long-Term Care Insurance (LTCI) reimbursement model. 

Because we specialize exclusively in non-medical daily living care and companionship rather than physician-ordered clinical nursing, our services are generally not covered by standard Medicare. However, we assist families with all documentation required for Long-Term Care Insurance, Veterans Aid & Attendance benefits, and private family arrangements.

## Care Arrangements & Scheduling
- **Hourly Care**: Flexible scheduling starting with standard 4-hour shifts up to 12-hour daytime coverage. Pay only for the exact hours your family needs, with no long-term lock-in contracts.
- **Respite Care**: Scheduled temporary care designed to give family caregivers essential rest, travel coverage, or relief during recovery periods.
- **Overnight Care**: Dedicated caregiver awake or on standby throughout the night to assist with bathroom transfers, wandering prevention, and peace of mind.
- **24/7 Round-the-Clock Care**: Continuous care coordinated through a consistent, matched team of caregivers for seniors requiring continuous oversight.
- **Complimentary In-Home Assessment**: $0 cost, zero-obligation consultation and home safety evaluation conducted by an experienced local care coordinator.

## Cost Comparison vs. Alternatives
- **Pure Heart In-Home Care**: Pay solely for hours utilized. Preserves familiar home routines, personal privacy, and one-on-one attention without expensive entry fees or facility room-and-board charges.
- **Assisted Living Facilities**: Often requires fixed monthly fees averaging $5,000 to $7,500+ across the Chicago metropolitan area, plus community buy-in fees, with staff shared across multiple residents.
- **Private Independent Hires**: May appear lower on hourly wage, but places 100% legal liability, payroll taxes, workers' compensation, screening risks, and absent-caregiver coverage directly onto the family.
