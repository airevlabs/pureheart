Care Definition

What is non-medical home care?

Non-medical home care is professional in-home assistance that helps seniors and aging adults safely perform activities of daily living—including bathing, dressing, meal preparation, light housekeeping, and companion care. Unlike clinical home health nursing, it focuses on daily independence, comfort, and personal dignity without invasive medical procedures.

Personal Care & Daily Living

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and mobility assistance, delivered with patience and dignity.

Companionship Care

Frequently Asked Questions About Home Care

Have questions about non-medical home care, scheduling, costs, or caregiver screening? Here is everything families need to know when choosing care across Chicago, Rockford, and Illinois suburbs.

What is the difference between non-medical home care and skilled home health?

Non-medical home care assists seniors with activities of daily living (ADLs)—such as bathing, grooming, dressing, meal preparation, medication reminders, companionship, and mobility support. Unlike skilled clinical home health, it does not involve medical nursing, wound care, or IV therapies, allowing seniors to remain safe, comfortable, and independent at home.

Does Medicare or private health insurance pay for in-home personal care?

Traditional Medicare and standard health insurance typically only cover short-term, doctor-prescribed clinical home health care. Non-medical daily home care is generally covered through private pay, long-term care insurance (LTCI), Veterans Aid and Attendance benefits, or specific Medicaid waiver programs. Pure Heart helps families explore all eligible coverage options.

What areas throughout Illinois does Pure Heart serve?

Pure Heart provides compassionate in-home care across the City of Chicago, Rockford, and surrounding northern Illinois suburbs. Our care network extends throughout Cook, DuPage, Lake, Will, Kane, McHenry, and Winnebago Counties, pairing families with dedicated, background-checked local caregivers in their immediate neighborhoods.

How quickly can caregiver support begin after an inquiry?

Following an initial conversation and a complimentary in-home consultation and safety assessment, Pure Heart can typically match a dedicated caregiver and begin care within 24 to 48 hours. We also accommodate urgent family transitions, sudden caregiver absences, and immediate hospital or rehabilitation discharge plans.

Are Pure Heart caregivers licensed, background-checked, and insured?

Yes. Pure Heart Home Health Care is licensed by the State of Illinois and fully bonded and insured. 100% of our professional caregivers undergo rigorous multi-state criminal background checks, motor vehicle screenings, credential verifications, and structured personal interviews before ever entering a client's home.

What happens if our regular caregiver is sick or unavailable?

Pure Heart provides 24/7 care coordination and maintains a network of thoroughly vetted backup caregivers. If your primary caregiver is ill, taking planned time off, or encounters an emergency, our care coordination team immediately coordinates a qualified replacement caregiver to ensure uninterrupted care and peace of mind.

Can care schedules and hours change as my loved one's needs evolve?

Yes. Pure Heart builds completely customizable and flexible care plans. Whether your family needs companion visits a few hours per week, respite relief, 12-hour daytime blocks, or around-the-clock 24/7 live-in support, our care coordinators regularly review and adjust schedules as your loved one's condition changes.

How do you match a caregiver with my loved one?

Caregiver matching at Pure Heart goes beyond routine scheduling. During our free in-home consultation, we assess your loved one's specific physical needs, daily routines, dietary preferences, personality traits, and hobbies. We then carefully introduce a compatible caregiver to ensure a warm, trusting, and family-like relationship.
