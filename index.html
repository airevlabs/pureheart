The Challenge Every Family Faces

The Home Care Challenges Chicagoland Families Face

Between doctor's appointments, phone calls, and worry, caregiving for an aging parent in Chicago, Rockford, or the suburbs can feel like a second job — one nobody trained you for.

Too much to manage alone

Daily routines, mobility risks, and household tasks pile up faster than one family can safely handle.

Not sure who to trust

Inviting a stranger into a parent's home is a leap of faith most agencies don't earn.

Afraid of losing independence

The fear that asking for help means giving up the home and routines your loved one built a life around.

How Pure Heart Helps

One trusted team. A plan built around your family.

Pure Heart pairs your loved one with a consistent, background-checked caregiver and a personalized plan of care — so daily support feels like family, not a revolving door of strangers. Pure Heart provides dedicated home care; we do not provide skilled nursing or clinical medical services.

Personal Care & Daily Living

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and mobility assistance, delivered with patience and dignity.

Companionship Care

Conversation, meals, errands, and presence — because isolation is one of the hardest parts of aging alone.

Respite Care

Scheduled relief for family caregivers, from a few hours a week to overnight coverage.

Alzheimer's & Dementia Care

Caregivers trained in specialized memory-care routines, redirection techniques, and home safety.

Homemaker & Light Housekeeping

Meal preparation, laundry, tidying, and keeping the home safe and comfortable.

Transportation & Errands

Rides to appointments, grocery runs, and errands, with a familiar face along the way.
